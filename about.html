ABOUT THE PROJECT

A device to build, an idea to share.

Make2Hear is an open source and open hardware project for building an affordable DIY listening device.

Here you'll find a component list, firmware, an enclosure model, and an assembly guide. The current firmware and enclosure files are placeholders and will be replaced with the final versions.
